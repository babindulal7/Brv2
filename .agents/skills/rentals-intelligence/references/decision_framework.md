# Babin Rentals — Operational Decision Framework (Brain v4)

> *"Rental Intelligence must not try to sound intelligent. It must produce decisions that are traceable, measurable, reversible, and improved by actual rental outcomes."*

---

## 1. THE CANONICAL 7-STEP REASONING PATTERN

Every strategic recommendation, substitution, risk assessment, or pricing decision generated by the system MUST follow this strict 7-step structure:

```
Situation:       [Brief / Request / Client history / Location context]
Evidence:        [Historical data, lab benchmarks, physical stock, payment records]
Constraints:     [OS Laws (Law 5, Law 10), physical limits, power/weight budgets]
Options:         [Option A vs Option B vs Option C with explicit trade-offs]
Recommendation:  [Definitive action or kit configuration with package economics]
Confidence:      [High / Medium / Low + Tier A/B/C/D Source Provenance]
Required Action: [Specific approval gate, booking status change, or deposit hold]
```

## 2. PRODUCTION SCENARIO WALKTHROUGHS

### Scenario 1: Shoot Intake & Package Synthesis
* **Situation:** Returning client (CL-014, Pana Weddings) requests an FX3 package for a 3-day luxury wedding at Gokarna Forest Resort. Budget is flexible; high autofocus and low-light performance are critical.
* **Evidence:**
  - Client rented FX3A 14 times with zero damage history (Trusted Tier).
  - Co-occurrence data (`data/rental_patterns.json`) shows FX3A pairs with 24-70 GM II in 68% of bookings and RS3 Pro in 52%.
  - CineD lab benchmark: FX3 rolling shutter is 8.7ms (excellent for fast handheld dancing).
* **Constraints:**
  - Law 1: Business logic owned by domain engines.
  - §R4: FX3 requires CFexpress Type A, card reader, 3x NP-FZ100, and ZG charger.
  - Multi-day rule: Days 1-4 standard linear rate; multi-day discount applies from Day 5+.
* **Options:**
  - *Option A (À-la-carte):* FX3A body (Rs 3,500/d) + 24-70 GM II (Rs 2,000/d) + RS3 Pro (Rs 2,000/d) = Rs 7,500/day.
  - *Option B (Creator Bundle):* Creator Kit (FX3A + 24-70 GM + RS3 Pro + DJI Mic Mini + 2 cards + 3 batts) @ Rs 6,800/day (saves Rs 1,200/day vs separate).
  - *Option C (Dual-Cam Upgrade):* 2x FX3A + 24-70 GM + 70-200 GM @ Rs 12,500/day.
* **Recommendation:** Recommend Option B (Creator Bundle) as primary, with Option C as a single upsell delta: *"For Rs 5,700 more/day, add a second matching FX3A + 70-200 GM II for full ceremony coverage without cutting angles."*
* **Confidence:** High (Tier A - Babin fleet transaction history).
* **Required Action:** Reserve FX3A #002 and RS3 Pro #001; require 50% advance (Rs 10,200) to confirm `Booked` status (§R1).

### Scenario 2: Equipment Conflict & Technical Substitution
* **Situation:** Indie filmmaker requests Sony FX3A for tomorrow morning, but both units are currently in `On Rent` status through the weekend.
* **Evidence:**
  - Live availability engine confirms 0 FX3A available.
  - Sony A7S III is `AVAILABLE` (inspected and cleaned in ready cabinet).
  - CineD lab benchmarks show FX3 and A7S III share identical 12MP BSI sensors, dual-native ISOs 800/12800, and 8.7ms rolling shutter.
  - Operational difference: A7S III uses standard mirrorless body without top handle XLR audio inputs or active cooling fan.
* **Constraints:**
  - §2.5 & §9: Never invent stock; offer honest downsell or upgrade at delta price; never perform a silent swap.
  - Law 17: Address as client.
* **Options:**
  - *Option A (Direct Downsell):* Sony A7S III at Rs 3,000/day (save Rs 500/day vs FX3).
  - *Option B (Cinema Upgrade):* RED Komodo 6K Cinema Package at Rs 8,000/day (+Rs 4,500/day).
  - *Option C (Turn Away):* Reject booking outright.
* **Recommendation:** Offer Option A (A7S III) with transparent technical reasoning: *"Our FX3A units are on set until Monday. We can offer the Sony A7S III for Rs 3,000/day (saving Rs 500/day). The sensor, low-light performance, and color science are 100% identical. The only difference is the physical body layout and internal mic inputs."*
* **Confidence:** High (Tier B - Sony manufacturer spec + Tier C CineD lab tests).
* **Required Action:** If client accepts, draft quote for A7S III and hold serial #A7S-001.

### Scenario 3: High-Risk Mountain Expedition (Mustang / Manang)
* **Situation:** Client requests FX3 + Tamron 70-180 for a 5-day trekking documentary in Upper Mustang (elevation 3,800m, forecasted temps -8°C).
* **Evidence:**
  - Field Sop (`references/extreme_weather.md`): Lithium-ion battery capacity drops ~40% at sub-zero temperatures.
  - Extreme dust and wind in Mustang valley during afternoon hours.
  - Zero camera supply stores exist beyond Pokhara.
* **Constraints:**
  - Redundancy doctrine (§5): Minimum 1 backup battery system, 1 backup card, 1 backup cable.
  - Multi-day pricing: Day 5 qualifies for 10% discount on incremental day.
* **Options:**
  - *Standard Kit:* FX3 + 3x NP-FZ100 + 1x 160GB CFexpress card.
  - *Hardened Expedition Kit:* FX3 + 4x NP-FZ100 + 1x 99Wh V-Mount battery with D-Tap dummy battery + 2x 160GB cards + card reader + sealed Pelican case + silica gel packs.
* **Recommendation:** Strictly mandate the Hardened Expedition Kit. Explain the operational reality: *"At 3,800m in Mustang, NP-FZ100 runtime drops from 110 minutes to under 50 minutes. The V-Mount battery operates safely down to -10°C and powers your base rig all day."*
* **Confidence:** High (Tier A - Babin field expedition data + Outcome Log REC-2026-003).
* **Required Action:** Auto-add V-Mount plate, V-Mount battery, and Pelican hard case; mandate 100% full payment before dispatch (§R1).

### Scenario 4: Discount Request & Law 10 Gate Enforcement
* **Situation:** Film student asks for a 20% discount on a 2-day booking of the Blazar Mantis Anamorphic lens set (Rental total: Rs 15,000).
* **Evidence:**
  - Client has a valid student ID card, but no official college recommendation letter.
  - §R2 Policy: Student with valid ID gets 10% (à-la-carte only). College project with official letter gets 20%. Bundles get 0% student discount without owner sign-off.
* **Constraints:**
  - Law 10 Gate: Discounts > 10% REQUIRE reason >= 10 characters AND named Manager or Owner approver.
  - Law 10 Hard Cap: Maximum 25% discount, zero role bypass.
* **Options:**
  - *Option A:* Grant 20% immediately (VIOLATION of Law 10 and §R2 policy).
  - *Option B:* Apply standard 10% student discount (Rs 1,500 off) with valid ID card.
  - *Option C:* Inform client that 20% requires an official recommendation letter signed by their campus department head.
* **Recommendation:** Execute Option B (10% student discount, save Rs 1,500) and provide the pathway to 20%: *"With your student ID, we are happy to apply our standard 10% student discount. If this is a registered campus project, bringing an official letter from your department allows us to upgrade the discount to 20%."*
* **Confidence:** High (Tier A - Babin Rentals OS Law 10 database constraints).
* **Required Action:** Apply 10% student discount in `os_engine.py`; log student ID number on booking file.

### Scenario 5: Damage Finding & Deposit Settlement
* **Situation:** Client returns NiSi Athena 35mm cine lens with a noticeable front element scratch and sand inside the focus ring gear after a beach/river shoot. Security deposit held: Rs 75,000.
* **Evidence:**
  - Prep tech photographs scratch and records grit grind in focus throw.
  - Repair estimate from local optics specialist: Polishing / replacement front element + deep sonic cleaning = Rs 28,000.
* **Constraints:**
  - Law 5: Deposits are liabilities; damage deduction must be transparently reconciled.
  - Damage SOP: Charge >= Rs 25,000 requires **Owner** sign-off (Manager signs < Rs 25,000).
  - Damage rule: Photos >= 1, description >= 10 characters.
  - Refund formula: `Refund = max(0, deposit - damage)`.
* **Options:**
  - *Option A:* Forfeit the entire Rs 75,000 deposit (VIOLATION of Law 5 and client trust).
  - *Option B:* Deduct verified Rs 28,000 damage fee, refund remaining Rs 47,000 to client.
* **Recommendation:** Execute Option B. Transparently settle ledger:
  - Deposit Held: Rs 75,000
  - Damage & Restoration Fee: Rs 28,000
  - Refund to Client: Rs 47,000
  - Escalate to Owner (Babin) for mandatory sign-off as charge exceeds Rs 25,000 threshold.
* **Confidence:** High (Tier A - Babin OS damage engine `damage.ts`).
* **Required Action:** Submit damage report with photo proof; obtain Owner authorization; issue Rs 47,000 refund via eSewa/bank transfer; move lens to `MAINTENANCE` status.

## 3. PRE-FLIGHT SELF-AUDIT CHECKLIST

Before ANY recommendation, quote, or booking confirmation is displayed to staff or client, run this mental audit:

| # | Check | Validation Question | Pass Condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Inventory Reality** | Is every single piece of gear in the Babin Rentals catalog? | No invented items (Blackmagic, Nanlite, Teradek = ❌) |
| **2** | **Availability Gate** | Is physical status `AVAILABLE`? | `RETURNED`, `HOLD`, `MONITOR`, `INSPECTION` = ❌ |
| **3** | **Kit Completeness** | Does the kit pass `complete_kit()` in `os_engine.py`? | 0 blocking errors; all required adapters included |
| **4** | **Payload Headroom** | Does rig weight have $\ge 30\%$ headroom on tripod / gimbal? | Rig weight $\le 70\%$ of tripod capacity |
| **5** | **Power Adequacy** | Are wattages within 15A/230V limits and runtimes calculated? | Run-time covers shoot duration + 1 backup batt |
| **6** | **Law 5 Deposit** | Is the deposit strictly separated as a liability? | Deposit does NOT reduce Rental Total or Outstanding |
| **7** | **Law 10 Discount** | Is discount $\le 10\%$, or backed by $\ge 10$-char reason + approver? | Never exceeds 25% hard cap under any circumstance |
| **8** | **Language & Tone** | Is the client addressed as Sir/Ma'am with no mention of "customer"? | Law 17 compliant |

## 4. PREPARATION TIME STANDARDS (Warehouse Staging)

To prevent rushed dispatches and skipped bench checks, every booking carries an estimated staging time:

| Package Category | Prep Duration | Mandatory QC Tasks Before Release |
|---|---|---|
| **Small Package** (Mirrorless + 1 Lens) | **15–30 mins** | Battery voltage test, sensor blower sweep, optics front/rear check |
| **Camera Kit** (FX3A + Gimbal + Mic) | **30–45 mins** | 45-degree gimbal motor balance, wireless mic channel sync, card format test |
| **Cinema Package** (RED / Ronin 4D / PL) | **1–2 hours** | 12G-SDI ground loop protocol, flange depth Siemens star check, D-Tap rail voltage |
| **Lighting Package** (600x / 300c / C-Stands) | **1–3 hours** | 10-minute continuous burn-in, Bowens locking pin test, 10kg sandbag counts |
| **Car Rig / Movement System** | **2–4 hours** | Suction cup vacuum retention test, dual-tether tensile inspection, dry run video |
| **Full Film Multi-Cam Package** | **Half Day (4–6 hrs)** | Multi-camera timecode sync, S-Log/REDCODE color match, complete crew handover |

5. **Two Staff Required (Commercial Van):** Car rig, 10x10 skimmer frame, Dana Dolly track, generator. (2 staff mandatory).

- [ ] **Gimbal Quick-Release Plate:** RS3/RS4 baseplate checked and fitted to camera cage.
- [ ] **Card Readers:** High-speed USB-C reader matching CFexpress Type A or B media packed.
- [ ] **Wireless Video Antennas:** TX and RX antenna counts verified (count: Accsoon 4x, Hollyland 2x).

To prevent catastrophic gear destruction, map client skill level against equipment limits:

* **Beginner:** Allowed: Sony A7 IV, Samyang V-AF, DJI Mic Mini, Amaran tubes. **HARD BLOCKED:** RED cinema bodies, Ronin 4D, CHIOPT zooms, Anamorphic lenses, Car rigs. *Override requires Camera Caretaker (Rs 2,000/day) or mandatory 15-minute operational briefing.*
* **Intermediate:** Allowed: Sony FX3A, A7S III, Sigma 24-70 II, DJI RS gimbals, Amaran 300c. *Review wireless link setup and gimbal balance.*
* **Professional:** Full catalog access. *Verify deposit authorization.*
* **Expert Crew Required:** Ronin 4D, CHIOPT cine zooms, Car rigs, Tilta Float, Aputure 600x. *Requires named DoP or AC on booking contract.*
