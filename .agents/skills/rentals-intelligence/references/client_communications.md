## 3. The 4-Stage Due Recovery Sequence

For managing the outstanding balances (e.g., 34 clients with overdue balances).

### Stage 1: Courtesy Reminder (Day 1–3 Post-Rental)
*Tone:* Friendly, collaborative, informal.
```text
Namaste [Client Name] ji, 
Asha cha tapai ko shoot ramro bhayo! Tapai ko Babin Rentals account ma NPR [Amount] ko balance settle garna baki cha. 
Fursad huna sath Fonepay (9712052571) marfat clear garidinuhola hai. Thank you so much!
```

### Stage 2: Formal Statement of Account (Day 7 Post-Rental)
*Tone:* Professional, clear financial statement.
```text
Dear [Client Name],

This is an official statement from the accounts department at Babin Rentals Pvt. Ltd. 
According to our records, Invoice #[Invoice No] for your rental on [Date] has an outstanding balance of NPR [Amount].

Please find our bank details below for immediate settlement:
• Account: Babin Rentals Pvt. Ltd.
• PAN: 623587106
• Fonepay: 9712052571

Kindly confirm once transfer is completed or inform us of your expected payment date. Thank you.
```

### Stage 3: Production Booking Freeze Notice (Day 14 Post-Rental)
*Tone:* Firm, policy enforcement, credit suspension.
```text
Namaste [Client Name] ji,

Hami le tapai ko baki balance NPR [Amount] ko barema 2 patak samparka gareka thiyau. 
Babin Rentals ko financial policy anusar 14 din bhanda dherai baki bhayeko account ma naya gear booking temporarily HOLD ma rakhincha.

Tapai ko upcoming projects ko rental smoothly chalauna ko lagi, kripaya yo balance 48 hours bhitra settle garidinuhola. 

Support ko lagi sidhai 9712052571 ma call garna saknuhuncha.
```

### Stage 4: Formal Legal Notice & Industry Blacklist (Day 30+ Post-Rental)
*Tone:* Strict legal recovery.
