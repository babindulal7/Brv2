# Cinema & Photographic Lenses Technical Reference

Detailed optical characteristics, mount adapters, front diameters, and mandatory support requirements for Babin Rentals lens fleet.

---

## 1. Anamorphic Systems

### Blazar Mantis 1.33x Anamorphic 5-Lens Set (ARRI PL)
- **Focal Lengths & Speeds:** 25mm T2.4, 35mm T2.2, 50mm T2.0, 75mm T2.4, 100mm T2.8.
- **Squeeze Factor:** 1.33x horizontal squeeze.
  - *Sensor Matching:* Designed specifically for 16:9 native sensors (Sony FX3, RED Komodo). 16:9 (1.78:1) multiplied by 1.33 equals **2.37:1 widescreen**, utilizing 100% of sensor resolution without black-bar cropping.
- **Optics & Mechanics:** Full-frame 36mm image circle; 80mm front diameter; 77mm front filter thread; 0.8 MOD focus/iris gears with 270° focus throw.
- **Weight:** 750g to 880g per lens. Can balance on DJI RS 4 Pro with counterweight.
- **Required Adapters:** PL-to-E (for Sony FX3/A7) or PL-to-RF (for RED Komodo/X).

### Blazar Great Joy 1.8x T2.9 Anamorphic Lens Set (PL)
- **Focal Lengths:** 35mm, 50mm, 85mm T2.9.
- **Squeeze Factor:** 1.8x (intense anamorphic aesthetic: pronounced oval bokeh, horizontal blue streak flares).
- **Weight:** ~1.1 kg each. Front heavy. Requires lens support on rods when using follow focus motors.

## 2. Par-Focal Cinema Zooms

### CHIOPT Xtreme Zoom 28-85mm T3.2 & 75-250mm T3.2 (PL Mount)
- **Coverage:** 46mm image circle (Full-Frame, Large Format, VistaVision).
- **Front Diameter:** 114mm front OD (requires 114mm clamp on matte box or step-up rings).
- **Weights:** 28-85mm weighs **2.8 kg (6.17 lb)**; 75-250mm weighs **3.1 kg (6.83 lb)**.
- **SAFETY MANDATE (NON-NEGOTIABLE):**
  - **Must require 15mm studio baseplate, 8-inch steel/carbon rods, and rigid lens support bracket.**
  - *Reason:* Mounting 3kg unsupported creates destructive bending torque on the camera mount, stripping mount screws or fracturing the magnesium flange.
- **Tripod Requirement:** Studio Assist Heavy Tripod (15kg rating). Do NOT mount on lightweight K&F travel tripods.

## 3. Cinema Prime Sets

### NiSi Athena Prime 5-Lens Set (PL Mount)
- **Focal Lengths:** 14mm T2.4, 24mm T1.9, 35mm T1.9, 50mm T1.9, 85mm T1.9.
- **Gimbal Workflow Advantage:** 24mm, 35mm, 50mm, and 85mm share the **identical 800g weight, 80mm front diameter, and matched gear spacing**. Operators can hot-swap focal lengths on the DJI RS 4 Pro without needing to re-balance the gimbal axes!
- **Filter Thread:** 77mm screw-in front thread.

## 4. Autofocus Zoom & Prime Lenses (Sony E-Mount)

### Sigma 24-70mm f/2.8 DG DN II Art
- **Weight:** 745g (90g lighter than predecessor).
- **Autofocus:** Dual HLA (High-response Linear Actuator) motors provide instant eye-tracking on FX3.
- **Filter Thread:** 82mm front filter thread.
- **Best Use Case:** Run-and-gun wedding, event, and gimbal tracking shots.

### Samyang V-AF Series (24mm, 35mm, 45mm, 75mm T1.9)
- **Design:** Ultra-lightweight cine-autofocus primes (~280g each).
- **Front Diameter:** 70mm front with 58mm filter thread. Built-in tally lamp for video creators.
- **Best Use Case:** Solo operators on DJI RS 3 / RS 5 gimbals needing autofocus and all-day fatigue reduction.
