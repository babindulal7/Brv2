# Video Monitoring & Wireless Video Reference

Display calibrations, latency standards, antenna protocols, and wireless routing for Babin Rentals monitoring fleet.

---

## 1. Integrated Wireless Systems

### Accsoon CineView M7 Pro 7" High-Bright Monitor
- **Display:** 7-inch IPS panel, 1920x1200 resolution, **1200 nits brightness** (direct sunlight readable without heavy sunhood).
- **Integrated Wireless Engine:** Contains built-in dual-band 2.4GHz and 5GHz wireless transceiver. Can function as a **Transmitter (TX)** or a **Receiver (RX)** via on-screen software toggle.
- **Range & Latency:** Line-of-sight range up to **350 meters (1,200 ft)** with ultra-low latency of **<35ms**.
- **Inputs & Outputs:**
  - 1x 3G-SDI Input, 1x 3G-SDI Loop Output.
  - 1x HDMI Input, 1x HDMI Loop Output.
- **Power Draw:** 10 Watts. Can be powered via single Sony NP-F970 (~4.0 hrs runtime) or D-Tap DC in (14.8V V-Mount yields ~8.4 hrs).
- **Antenna Protocol (Loss Prevention Gate):**
  - Requires **4x external RP-SMA dual-band paddle antennas**.
  - *SOP Check:* Antennas must be unscrewed and packed into the designated foam case slot during return. Missing antennas are flagged immediately and billed at NPR 1,500 each.

### Accsoon CineView SDI 2 (TX & RX System)
- **Design:** Dedicated standalone transmitter and receiver pair.
- **Ports:** 3G-SDI in/out + HDMI in/out. Dual-band signal hopping eliminates Wi-Fi interference on busy film sets.
- **Power:** Low power consumption (4.5W TX / 3.5W RX). Powered via NP-F series, USB-C 5V, or DC barrel jack.

## 2. On-Camera Recording Monitors

### Atomos Ninja V+ (5.2" 8K RAW Recorder)
- **Screen:** 5.2-inch 1920x1080 10-bit HDR display, 1000 nits brightness.
- **Recording Capability:**
  - Records up to **8K 30p and 4K 120p Apple ProRes RAW** from compatible cameras over HDMI (Sony FX3, A7S III).
  - Also encodes Apple ProRes 422 and Avid DNxHR to standard SATA SSDs in Master Caddy II.
- **Power:** 10W to 22W peak during high-resolution recording. Powered by NP-F or D-Tap dummy battery.

### Desview OLED Director Monitors (14-inch & 16-inch)
- **Display:** True OLED panel delivering infinite contrast ratio (pure black) and 100% DCI-P3 color coverage.
- **Best Use Case:** High-end commercial and cinema director stations where color grading accuracy and exposure ratios are evaluated on set.
- **Video Inputs:** 12G-SDI / 3G-SDI in + HDMI in with cross-conversion pass-through.
- **Power:** Dual V-Mount plate integrated on back.
