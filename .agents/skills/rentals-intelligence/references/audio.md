# Professional Audio & Intercom Technical Reference

Wireless lavaliers, shotgun microphones, multi-person crew intercom systems, and camera input routing for Babin Rentals audio fleet.

---

## 1. Crew Wireless Intercom Systems

### Accsoon CoMo / CoMo SE Headset Intercom (5-Person & 9-Person Sets)
- **Architecture:** Full-duplex wireless intercom (all operators can speak and listen simultaneously without pushing buttons).
- **Frequency:** 1.9GHz / 2.4GHz digital encryption (avoids crowded 5GHz video transmission bands).
- **Range:** Up to **400 meters (1,300 ft)** line-of-sight.
- **Battery Life:** 10 to 13 hours continuous talk time per headset.
- **Role Routing:** Master headset (Director/DP) + Remote headsets (Gaffer, 1st AC, Sound recordist, Production Manager).

### Hollyland Solidcom C1 (8-Person System)
- **Transmission:** DECT 6.0 1.9GHz technology.
- **Battery:** Dual battery packs per headset included in charging hub (~10 hrs runtime per battery).

---

## 2. Wireless Lavalier Systems

### DJI Mic Mini / DJI Mic 2
- **Transmission:** 2.4GHz digital frequency-hopping technology.
- **Range:** Up to 250 meters line-of-sight.
- **Receiver Connections:** USB-C (for phones), Lightning adapter, 3.5mm TRS cable for camera mic input.
- **Internal Recording (DJI Mic 2):** 32-bit float internal backup recording prevents clipping and distorted audio during unexpected shouting or loud environments.

---

## 3. Shotgun Microphones & Camera Interfaces

### RODE VideoMic NTG (Hybrid Analog/USB Shotgun)
- **Acoustic Design:** Pressure gradient electret condenser with supercardioid polar pattern.
- **Outputs:** Auto-sensing 3.5mm TRRS output (detects camera vs mobile device automatically), USB-C digital output.
- **Battery:** Built-in rechargeable lithium-ion battery delivering **30+ hours** continuous operation.
- **Safety Precaution:** Ensure included high-frequency boost and -20dB pad settings match the acoustic environment.

### Sony XLR-H1 Top Handle (Included with FX3)
- **Inputs:** 2x standard 3-pin XLR/TRS combo inputs with switchable +48V phantom power and line/mic gain switches.
- **Connection:** Mounts directly to Sony Multi-Interface (MI) shoe on FX3/A7 top plate, transferring 24-bit 4-channel digital audio without external analog cables.
