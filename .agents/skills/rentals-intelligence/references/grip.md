# Support, Gimbals & Grip Technical Reference

Payload thresholds, head configurations, slider capacities, vehicle rigging, and physical safety margins for Babin Rentals grip equipment.

---

## 1. Motorized Gimbals

### DJI RS 4 Pro
- **Tested Payload Capacity:** 4.5 kg (10.0 lb).
- **Recommended Operational Working Payload:** **3.8 kg (8.3 lb)**.
  - *Dynamic Safety Margin:* Preserves 15.5% headroom to prevent motor oscillation during high-speed tracking or vehicle mounts.
- **Motor Improvements:** 20% torque increase over RS 3 Pro on all three axes; Teflon-coated carbon fiber arm extensions.
- **Balancing Protocol:** Must achieve mechanical equilibrium at 45° tilt, roll, and pan with motors completely unpowered before switching on.

### DJI RS 3
- **Tested Payload Capacity:** 3.0 kg (6.6 lb).
- **Recommended Operational Working Payload:** **2.4 kg (5.3 lb)**.
- **Best Pairing:** Sony FX3 or A7 IV with Sigma 24-70mm Art II or Samyang V-AF primes. Not compatible with heavy cinema glass or V-Mount battery rigs.

## 2. Heavy-Duty Sliders & Jibs

### Dana Dolly NS-50 Slider System
- **Payload Capacity:** Up to **45 kg (100 lb)**.
- **Design:** 16-wheel custom polyurethane truck riding on standard aluminum or steel pipes.
- **Bowl Adapters Included:** 75mm, 100mm, and 150mm bowl adapters + Mitchell mount plate.
- **Stand Requirement:** Must be supported by 2x Low Boy Junior Stands or Baby stands with pipe clamps.

### Came-TV Hydraulic Suspension Car Rig & NSH Rig
- **Payload Capacity:** Up to 15 kg.
- **Shock Absorption:** Hydraulic damping cylinder mitigates road bumps and engine vibrations.
- **Mounting Protocol:**
  - 3x heavy-duty vacuum suction cups (pump indicator line must stay recessed).
  - **MANDATORY SAFETY TETHER:** Steel safety cable must tether the rig directly to a structural vehicle point (tow hook, roof rail). Never operate without safety tether.

## 3. Tripods & Rigging Headroom (The 30% Headroom Law)

All tripod systems must maintain at least **30% safety headroom** below maximum manufacturer payload:

1. **Studio Assist Heavy Tripod:**
   - Maximum Payload: 15.0 kg.
   - Recommended Working Load: **10.5 kg**.
   - *Application:* RED Komodo-X, CHIOPT Zooms, fully rigged studio builds.
2. **K&F Concept VA3 Fluid Head Tripod:**
   - Maximum Payload: 10.0 kg.
   - Recommended Working Load: **7.0 kg**.
   - *Application:* Sony FX3 run-and-gun packages, documentary setups.
