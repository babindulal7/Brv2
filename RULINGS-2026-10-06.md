# Rulings - 6 October 2026

Provisional build-time choices from the second pass over consistent Add / Edit / Booking workflows (continues the 2 October delivery, migrations 0146-0147). Babin may flip a row after review.

| # | Conflict or gap | Decision | Why | Changed where | Revert |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Add Client used a rich form whose extra inputs (profession, segment, tier, documents) `createClient` never stored; Edit used a different form | Add and Edit Client are the one shared `RecordForm`. `AddClient.tsx` is kept (Law 3) but no longer routed | `createClient` persists exactly the fields `RecordForm` edits, so nothing stored is lost and no input silently discards a value | `Clients.tsx`, `RecordForm.tsx` | Re-point `Clients.tsx` `tab=new` at `AddClient` |
| 2 | Client detail "Edit Client" opened a per-field inline editor with a "session only" banner; standing-discount keys were dropped by `updateClient` | `Edit Client` opens `?action=edit` (shared form, one **Save changes**). | 3 | Editor RPC wrote `client_status = 'Inactive'` (enum is `Active`/`Deactivated`), so removing or replacing a primary contact failed | Fixed in 0153. Found by new lifecycle step L25 | Verified by the database, not by reading | `0153_record_editor_fixes.sql` | n/a (bug fix) |
| 4 | `canCreateClient` includes Warehouse; the editor RPC did not | Warehouse may **create** a client through the editor; editing stays Owner/Admin/Manager/Coordinator. Public `read_record_editor` keeps its original gate; the save reads back through a private snapshot | Parity with `clients_insert`/`canCreateClient`, not a widening. Warehouse gains no read of other clients | 0153, `RecordForm.tsx` `rolesFor` | Remove `'Warehouse'` from the create array in 0153 and `rolesFor` |
| 5 | `updateBundle` issued header, line, add and deactivate writes separately and could half-save | `updateBundle` keeps its exported types and calls one `update_bundle` RPC (revision lock, idempotency receipt, soft-deactivation). `expectedRevision` is typed optional but **required by `validate`** | Atomic multi-table Save changes; existing callers still compile | 0153, `updateBundle.ts`, `AddBundle.tsx` | Restore the sequential body from git history |
| 6 | Equipment Add had been replaced by the generic form; the legacy seven-section form collects inputs (supplier, owner, relationships, images, insurance policy) that no column stores | Equipment Add and Edit stay on the supported-fields-only `RecordForm`. `LegacyAddEquipment` is kept and restorable | "Unsupported fields must not appear as inputs that silently discard values" | unchanged | Point `AddEquipment` default export back at `LegacyAddEquipment` |
