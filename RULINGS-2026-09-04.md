## R-01 — Invoice creation

Manual only, from **Finance** and from **Booking Detail**. Nothing generates an invoice at Confirm.

## R-02 — Invoice lifecycle

An invoice is created as **Draft** and stays Draft for the whole rental. It is **issued once, at return**, when the final figure is known.

## R-03 — Damage and late fees

Append as lines to the **Draft** invoice. One invoice per rental (21 Jul ruling, unchanged).

## R-04 — Corrections after issue

**Void + Credit Note.** Never a direct edit. This is the path `reject_invoice_edit_after_issue` already enforces.

## R-05 — Issue rights

Owner · Admin · Manager · Accounts may move an invoice out of Draft.

## R-06 — Handover money gate

**Warns, does not block.** Proceeding with an outstanding balance or an unpaid deposit requires a **named override reason**, recorded in `audit_events` with the amounts as they stood.

The deposit warning is the loud one: an unpaid rental invoice is a receivable, but a missing security deposit is the only cover against damage and non-return.

## R-07 — Close gate

**Stays a hard block.** Outstanding must be 0 and the deposit resolved. Auto-suggest when all gates pass; a human confirms.

## R-08 — Close confirmation rights

Same set as R-05: Owner · Admin · Manager · Accounts.

## R-09 — Reopen

**Stage-only.** The booking returns to *Returned*; the issued invoice is untouched. Further charges take Void + Credit Note (R-04). Restricted to **Owner · Admin**.

## R-10 — Booking numbers

Format is **`#<n>`** — bare integer, `#` rendered at display only. Never reset. The counter continues Booqable's order numbers: existing bookings keep #700–#899, the next real order is #946.

When the bridge ends, the sequence hands over to `next_document_number` continuing from the last imported number.

## R-11 — Seed corpus

The 46 seed bookings at #900–#945 are **renumbered to #90000+** by migration, freeing the range for real orders.

## R-12 — Other document prefixes

`INV-`, `BND-`, `CASE-`, `DEP-`, `EXP-`, `PAY-`, `Q-`, `C-`, `EQ` are unchanged. Only bookings drop their prefix.

## R-13 — Late fee

≤ 30 minutes: free. Under 2 hours: half day. 2 hours and over: full day.
Supersedes the "1h/2h" and "2h grace, 50% per part day" variants.

# Rulings forced by live-database findings, 4 Sep

These were not asked for. They are the minimum changes that make R-02, R-03 and R-09 implementable at all. Each was verified against the live project (`sxguwvgoserhdolewibb`) on 4 Sep.

## R-14 — Draft is sticky until explicitly issued

`sync_payment_to_invoice` currently flips an invoice to *Partially Paid* on the first payment. That would push the invoice out of Draft mid-rental, at which point `reject_invoice_edit_after_issue` starts protecting the figures and **appending damage lines at return would throw**. R-02 and R-03 are mutually impossible under the current trigger.

Ruling: while `status = 'Draft'`, a payment updates `paid_amount` and `outstanding_amount` but **does not change status**. Only an explicit issue action moves an invoice out of Draft.

## R-15 — `invoice_no` is minted at issue, not at creation

A number minted at Draft creation is lost if the draft is abandoned, leaving a gap in the tax-invoice sequence. Nepal IRD sequencing requires no gaps among issued invoices.

Ruling: a Draft carries no `invoice_no`. It is minted by `next_document_number('invoice')` at the moment of issue, inside the same transaction.

## R-16 — Closing sets a terminal flag that reopen must clear

`apply_booking_stage_transition` refuses **every** further transition once `bookings.closed_at` is set, and never clears it. Reversals are otherwise already supported (`is_reversal`), so R-09 is one clearing statement away rather than a new mechanism.

Ruling: an approved reversal out of *Closed* clears `closed_at` in the same statement that records the transition. Permitted only for Owner and Admin (R-09), and only with a reason.

## R-17 — Absolute discounts are not representable

`recalculate_invoice_totals` derives `discount_amount` from `discount_percent` and overwrites whatever was stored. A fixed-rupee discount cannot survive a line change.

Ruling: v1 supports **percentage discounts only**. `discount_amount` is a derived column and must not be written directly. A fixed-rupee discount is entered as the equivalent percentage or as a negative line.

## R-18 — Payment numbers stop resetting yearly

`number_sequences.payment` has `include_year = true`, so payment numbers reset each January — a direct breach of the 15 Aug never-reset ruling.

Ruling: set `include_year = false` for `payment`. Numbers already issued are not rewritten.
