# Rulings — 9 October 2026 New Rental redesign

User direction: “organize all buttons and functions in the easiest places to use, redesign the visuals, rearrange everything,” followed by instructions to continue. These are presentation and interaction changes to New Rental.

| Area | Implemented direction |
|---|---|
| Booking setup | Client and rental period form the full-width first row. Essential sections stay open. Client history remains inside the client search field; booking dates and times remain orange. |
| Navigation | A sticky section bar jumps to Client & dates, Equipment, Crew & delivery, Pricing, and Review. Pricing respects money visibility. Crew and delivery can collapse while preserving their values. |
| Equipment | Search, scan, and browse lead into the catalogue, followed by selected items and optional service, sub-rental, and custom charges. Forms open in view and focus their first field. |
| Phone catalogue | Categories stay in a horizontal tab row. Filters and sorting open in the same bounded panel, with “Show results” returning to the catalogue. Active filters persist. Panel height accounts for the header and action footer. |
| Pricing | Discounts and taxes precede the charge breakdown. Extra discount controls use a disclosure; validation remains visible. The refundable deposit stays separate from the rental total. |
| Review and actions | The summary shows the actual blocking requirements and direct links to fix them. One footer holds Cancel, save status, total, Save draft, and Confirm Booking; its phone layout clears the bottom navigation. |
| Popups and keyboard | Client results stay between the sticky navigation and footer. Confirm focuses the first missing field. Keyboard shortcuts respect busy state and the existing edit/save flow. |

No pricing engine, booking permission, custody transition, or database schema changes were made. Existing client ID/PAN advice remains non-blocking under the 8 October ruling. No booking was created during browser verification.
