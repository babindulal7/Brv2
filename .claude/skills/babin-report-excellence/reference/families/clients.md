# Family: Client / rental activity

## Question this report answers
"Who are our best clients, who is at risk, and where is repeat business?"

## KPI strip (pick 3–5)
Active clients (Δ) · Revenue from top client / top-3 concentration % · Repeat-booking rate · Avg booking value · New vs returning split.

## Sections that matter
- Top clients: ranked by revenue, with booking count and avg value — a comparison table; highlight top 3.
- Concentration risk: % of revenue from top 3 clients; call out if one leaving hurts materially.
- Standing discounts: named clients with their standing discount (20/13/10%) and volume — is the discount earning loyalty?
- Repeat & recency: repeat rate, last-booking recency; dormant good clients to re-activate.
- Client-tier / risk mix: A/B/C tiers and deposit behavior (10/30/100%); overdue-payment clients flagged.

## Trust rules
- Respect the standing-discount facts on file; don't recompute discounts, report them.
- Segment new vs returning explicitly; define "active" (booked in the last N months) in the appendix.
- Any risk/tier label is descriptive of recorded behavior, not a judgment about the person.

## Delta direction
Revenue, repeat rate, active clients: up = good. Concentration %, overdue clients, days-to-pay: lower = better (invert).
