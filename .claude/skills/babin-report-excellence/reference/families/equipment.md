# Family: Equipment (utilization / ROI)

## Question this report answers
"Which gear earns its keep, which sits idle, and what should we buy or retire?"

## KPI strip (pick 3–5)
Fleet utilization % (Δ) · Revenue per asset · Idle capital (value of gear rented < X%) · Top earner · Worst earner / retire candidate.

## Sections that matter
- Utilization by asset and by category — sorted horizontal bars, lowest utilization flagged.
- ROI per asset: revenue-to-date vs purchase/replacement value; payback status.
- Idle capital: assets below a utilization floor with their tied-up value — the buy/hold/retire shortlist.
- Maintenance drag: assets whose downtime (Maintenance/Reset) eats utilization; usage-hours + rental-count feed this.
- Purchase signal: high-demand/low-stock items (ties to Purchase Requests) as an opportunity list.

## Trust rules
- Utilization = rented days ÷ available days over the period; state the formula and window.
- Use replacement_value (defaults from purchase price) for ROI and idle-capital math; label which.
- Separate owned vs sub-rented; sub-rental margin (client price − supplier cost) is not asset ROI.

## Delta direction
Utilization, revenue per asset, ROI: up = good. Idle capital, downtime %, cost per rental: lower = better (invert).
