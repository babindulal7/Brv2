# Rulings — 13 October 2026

## Today controls and contextual phone search

The user's app-wide ease-of-use request and one-header acceptance plan require usable controls at phone and iPad widths.

- Today keeps one compact header creation control and More. Operations and Refresh movements use their existing handlers in More.
- The single dispatch date filter sits directly above its pickup/return lists, as a content filter. It controls both queues and retains AD/BS dates and existing business-day calculations. Counts and freshness stay with the content rather than competing for header width.
- Configured page searches remain available on phones through one contextual Search button. Its temporary search surface preserves the query on Escape, returns focus on dismissal, clears without closing, and uses the page's existing search and scan handlers. Pages with their own search portal retain ownership.
- Antigravity's Agency UI Designer, UX Architect and Evidence Collector guide consistency, responsive behavior and evidence collection. Existing product rules and the user's 64px single-header requirement take priority over generic design examples.

Revert: restore the Today header date/action group and remove MobileHeaderSearch from the shared header. No permission, money, booking state, database or custody rule changes.
