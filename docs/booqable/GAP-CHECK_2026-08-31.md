## THE GAPS

### P0 — data wiring (known, now with a checkable symptom)

The dashboards disagree with the live business that Booqable shows for the same minute:

| Figure | Our app | Live (Booqable, same day) |
|---|---|---|
| Incoming/returns due today (31 Aug) | **0** | **≥2** — #898 (3 picked up) and #903 (4 picked up) due back 31-08 06:00 |
| Rentals list total | 140 found | 653 bookings |
| Finance Received (Aug) | Rs 0 | 93 payments exist; Aug revenue Rs.838,665 |
| Deposit column | Rs 0 everywhere | (matches — deposits genuinely unset, D1) |

So the walked screens still read seed arrays or mis-scoped queries — the known 14-mixed-screens state. **The acceptance test writes itself now: our Dashboard is correct on the day it shows #898 and #903 due back, and Rentals says 653.** Wave 1 (Today/Dashboard + Operations + RentalsList onto `bookings.ts`) is the fix and was already the plan; D4's `operationalDayStart` setting lands in the same files.

### P1 — the rail pattern (spec: RAIL_AND_QUICK_ACTIONS §1, §3.1–3.2)

- No `FilterRail` exists. Rentals uses **two horizontal chip rows + a "Filters" button**; Dashboard shows two floating "More Filters" buttons (one is a duplicate); Inventory has status chips + "Clear Filters".
- **No facet counts anywhere except Operations' queue chips.** Needs `booking_facets()` — specced, unbuilt.
- The Rentals second chip row mixes types (Sub-Rental), time scopes (Tomorrow · Next 7 Days), states (Inspection · Completed) and money (Payment Due) in one strip — the grammar the rail spec orders as identity → lifecycle → dates → tags.
- Export/Import sit in page headers; the rail spec seats them as rail furniture (moot until a rail exists — move them when it lands).
- "Payment Due" chip renders **greyed** — if that grey is the money gate, the rule is *absent for money-blind roles, not disabled* (couldn't verify as Owner; check with a Warehouse session).

### P1 — quick actions (spec §2, §3.3–3.7)

- Booking Detail header shows a generic **"Operations" link + ⋮** — not the **single next-verb button** (F5/§10.1). B-701 at Returned should lead with its actual next verb (Reset/Close).
- Bulk bar exists on Inventory ("Bulk Actions", greyed at 0 — correct) but not on Rentals/Clients.
- Header primaries are right (`+ New Rental`, `+ Add Equipment`, `+ Record Payment` as Finance's primary); no split-create needed yet until sibling creates share a screen.

### P1 — date pickers (spec: DATE_TIME_PICKER v1.1)
